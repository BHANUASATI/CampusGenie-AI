# University Policies

## Attendance Policy

### Class Attendance Requirements
- Students are expected to attend all scheduled classes, laboratories, and examinations.
- Minimum attendance requirement: 75% of total classes for each course.
- Students falling below 75% attendance will receive a grade of 'F' regardless of academic performance.
- Attendance is recorded from the first day of classes.

### Excused Absences
Excused absences may be granted for:
- Medical emergencies (with documentation)
- Family emergencies (with documentation)
- University-sanctioned activities (athletics, academic competitions)
- Religious observances
- Military service

Students must submit excused absence requests within 48 hours of returning to class.

## Grading Policy

### Grade Scale
- A: 90-100% (Excellent)
- B: 80-89% (Good)
- C: 70-79% (Satisfactory)
- D: 60-69% (Passing)
- F: 0-59% (Failing)
- I: Incomplete (requires extenuating circumstances)
- W: Withdrawal (before deadline)

### Grade Point Average (GPA) Calculation
- A = 4.0 points
- B = 3.0 points
- C = 2.0 points
- D = 1.0 point
- F = 0.0 points

GPA is calculated by dividing total quality points by total credit hours attempted.

## Academic Integrity

### Prohibited Actions
- Plagiarism (submitting others' work as your own)
- Cheating on examinations
- Unauthorized collaboration
- Fabrication of data or sources
- Unauthorized use of AI tools without instructor permission

### Penalties
- First offense: F on assignment, formal warning
- Second offense: F in course, academic probation
- Third offense: Suspension or expulsion

## Student Conduct

### Code of Conduct
All students must:
- Respect the rights and dignity of others
- Maintain academic honesty
- Follow university rules and regulations
- Comply with local, state, and federal laws

### Disciplinary Process
Violations are reviewed by the Student Conduct Board. Penalties range from warnings to expulsion.

## Financial Policies

### Tuition Payment
- Tuition is due by the first day of classes each semester.
- Payment plans are available with a $50 setup fee.
- Late payment fee: $150 per month.
- Financial aid disbursement: 10 days before semester start.

### Refund Policy
- 100% refund if withdrawal before first day of classes
- 90% refund during first week
- 50% refund during second week
- No refund after second week
