DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER APPLICATIONS (MCA)
ATTENDANCE POLICY

Document Type: Academic Policy
Applicable To: MCA I, II, III Semester Students
Effective From: Academic Year 2025-26

1. MINIMUM ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
1.1 Every student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each theory and practical (lab) course during a semester.
1.2 Attendance is calculated separately for each subject and separately for lab sessions.
1.3 Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may be granted condonation by the Head of Department only on medical grounds, supported by valid medical certificates, subject to a maximum of two condonations during the entire course.
1.4 Students with attendance below 65% in any subject will be detained and will NOT be permitted to appear for the End Semester Examination of that subject.

2. CONDITIONS FOR SHORTAGE OF ATTENDANCE
2.1 Attendance shortage due to participation in university-approved sports, NSS/NCC camps, or technical competitions (with prior written approval from the Dean) will be counted as present for the days of absence, subject to submission of proof within 3 working days of return.
2.2 Medical leave exceeding 3 consecutive working days must be supported by a certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted to the class coordinator within 7 days.
2.3 Leave for personal reasons (family functions, personal work) is not counted towards condonation and will affect the attendance percentage.

3. ATTENDANCE CALCULATION
Attendance % = (Number of classes attended / Total number of classes held) x 100
This is calculated on a rolling basis and displayed on the student portal every Monday.

4. DEBARRING FROM EXAMINATION
4.1 A list of students detained due to shortage of attendance will be published on the notice board and student portal at least 10 days before the commencement of examinations.
4.2 Students detained for attendance shortage must repeat the course in the subsequent semester when it is offered.

5. SPECIAL PROVISIONS
5.1 Students representing the college in placement drives, internships approved by the Training & Placement Cell, or academic conferences will have such days counted as "on duty" and will not be deducted from attendance, provided prior approval is obtained.
5.2 Parents/guardians will be notified via SMS/email if a student's attendance falls below 70% in any subject at the mid-semester stage.

6. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: What is the minimum attendance criteria to sit in exams?
A: 75% attendance is mandatory. Between 65-74% requires medical condonation approval from HOD. Below 65% means the student is detained.

Q: Is attendance calculated subject-wise or overall?
A: Subject-wise (and lab-wise) attendance is tracked separately.

Q: Can I get condonation for a family function?
A: No, personal reasons other than medical emergencies are not eligible for condonation.

Q: How many times can attendance be condoned during the MCA program?
A: A maximum of two times, only on medical grounds.
