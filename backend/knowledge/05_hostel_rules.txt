HOSTEL RULES AND REGULATIONS
Applicable to all resident students (Boys' Hostel & Girls' Hostel)

1. TIMINGS
1.1 Hostel gate closing time: 9:30 PM (weekdays), 10:30 PM (weekends/holidays).
1.2 Late entry beyond gate closing time requires prior written permission from the Warden; unauthorized late entry will be recorded as a violation.
1.3 Mess timings: Breakfast 7:30-9:00 AM, Lunch 12:30-2:00 PM, Snacks 5:00-6:00 PM, Dinner 8:00-9:30 PM.

2. VISITORS
2.1 Visitors are allowed only in the common visiting area between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM on Saturdays and Sundays, with entry recorded in the visitor register and valid ID proof.
2.2 Visitors are not permitted inside student rooms under any circumstances.

3. LEAVE POLICY
3.1 Students must fill the Hostel Leave Form and get it countersigned by the Warden for any overnight leave.
3.2 Parents will be informed via SMS for any leave exceeding 2 days.
3.3 Home visits during mid-semester and end-semester exam periods are discouraged and require special approval.

4. DISCIPLINE
4.1 Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and will lead to immediate expulsion from the hostel and disciplinary action as per UGC anti-ragging regulations.
4.2 Consumption of alcohol, smoking, or any prohibited substances inside hostel premises will lead to immediate expulsion.
4.3 Use of electrical appliances such as heaters, induction cooktops in rooms is prohibited due to fire safety; only permitted appliances (laptop chargers, table lamps, mobile chargers) may be used.

5. FEES
5.1 Hostel fee (per semester, including mess): Rs. 45,000.
5.2 Security deposit (one-time, refundable): Rs. 10,000.
5.3 Mess rebate is applicable for continuous absence of more than 5 days with prior intimation, at Rs. 150/day.

6. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: What time does the hostel gate close?
A: 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends/holidays.
Q: Can visitors come to my room?
A: No, visitors are only allowed in the common visiting area on weekends, 4-7 PM.
Q: What is the hostel fee per semester?
A: Rs. 45,000 including mess charges, plus a one-time refundable security deposit of Rs. 10,000.
