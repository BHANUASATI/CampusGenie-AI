TRAINING AND PLACEMENT CELL (TPC)
PLACEMENT POLICY - MCA DEPARTMENT

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
1.1 Minimum 60% aggregate marks (or equivalent CGPA of 6.0) throughout the MCA program, with no active backlogs at the time of the placement drive.
1.2 Minimum 75% attendance as per the Attendance Policy.
1.3 Registration on the Placement Portal is mandatory by the start of the 3rd semester.

2. PLACEMENT PROCESS
2.1 Companies are invited to conduct pre-placement talks, followed by online assessments, technical interviews, and HR rounds as per company-specific process.
2.2 A student who accepts an offer through the "Dream Company" category (CTC above Rs. 12 LPA) will be debarred from further placement drives in that season.
2.3 A student who accepts a "Core Company" offer (CTC Rs. 6-12 LPA) can still sit for Dream Company drives but not for other Core Company drives.
2.4 Students in the "Mass Recruiter" category (CTC below Rs. 6 LPA) can appear in subsequent higher category drives.

3. INTERNSHIPS
3.1 A mandatory summer internship of minimum 6 weeks is required after Semester 2, to be completed before the start of Semester 3.
3.2 Internship report and certificate must be submitted to the TPC within 2 weeks of internship completion; this carries 50 marks as part of Semester 3 evaluation.

4. CODE OF CONDUCT FOR PLACEMENT
4.1 Students must not misrepresent academic records or fabricate resumes; violation leads to permanent debarment from campus placements.
4.2 Once a student clears the final round and receives a written offer, backing out without valid reason may lead to disciplinary action and a one-year ban from future placement drives for that student.

5. STATISTICS (Previous Batch)
Highest CTC: Rs. 24 LPA
Average CTC: Rs. 6.8 LPA
Placement Percentage: 87%
Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant, Amazon, Deloitte

6. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: What CGPA is required to sit for placements?
A: Minimum 6.0 CGPA (60% aggregate) with no active backlogs.
Q: Is internship mandatory?
A: Yes, a minimum 6-week internship after Semester 2 is compulsory and carries 50 marks in Semester 3.
Q: Can I sit for more drives after accepting a Dream Company offer?
A: No, once you accept a Dream Company offer you are debarred from further drives that season.
