DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER APPLICATIONS (MCA)
COMPUTER LABORATORY RULES AND TIMINGS

Document Type: Lab Guidelines
Applicable To: All MCA Lab Sessions (Programming Lab, Networking Lab, AI/ML Lab, Project Lab)

1. LAB TIMINGS
1.1 Regular Lab Hours (Monday to Friday): 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM
1.2 Each lab slot duration: 2 hours (as per timetable)
1.3 Extended/Open Lab access for project work: 4:30 PM to 7:00 PM (prior permission from lab in-charge required, entry logged in register)
1.4 Saturday Lab Access (only for final semester project students): 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM
1.5 Labs remain closed on Sundays and university holidays unless specially notified for hackathons or workshops.
1.6 Students must report to the lab at least 5 minutes before the scheduled time. Entry after 10 minutes of commencement will be marked late; after 15 minutes, entry is not allowed for that session.

2. GENERAL LAB RULES
2.1 Food and drinks (including tea, coffee, snacks, water bottles) are STRICTLY NOT ALLOWED inside the computer lab at any time. Students found consuming food/drinks near the systems will be fined Rs. 200 for the first offense and may be barred from the lab for repeated violations.
2.2 Footwear must be removed and kept in the designated rack before entering carpeted lab areas (Lab 3 and Lab 4 only); other labs require covered shoes.
2.3 Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode. Voice calls are not permitted inside the lab; students may step outside to attend calls.
2.4 Students must log in using their own credentials only. Sharing of login IDs is a violation of the Code of Conduct and may lead to disciplinary action.
2.5 Any hardware/software issue must be reported immediately to the lab assistant. Students are not permitted to open CPU cabinets or attempt hardware repairs themselves.
2.6 Personal USB drives/external storage must be scanned for viruses using the antivirus software installed on the lab system before use.
2.7 Downloading or installing unauthorized software, games, or torrents on lab systems is prohibited and may result in suspension of lab access.

3. PRACTICAL/PROJECT SUBMISSION IN LAB
3.1 All lab programs must be recorded in the lab record/journal and get it signed by the faculty in-charge within the same week.
3.2 Continuous absence from lab sessions (more than 3 consecutive sessions without prior intimation) will lead to a warning letter to the student and parent.

4. SAFETY AND MAINTENANCE
4.1 Students must shut down systems properly before leaving and push in chairs.
4.2 Any damage to lab equipment due to negligence will be charged to the student as per actual replacement/repair cost.
4.3 Fire extinguishers and emergency exits are marked in each lab; students must familiarize themselves with evacuation routes during the orientation session.

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: What are the normal lab timings?
A: Regular lab hours are 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM on weekdays, with each session lasting 2 hours as per the timetable.

Q: Can I bring food or water bottles into the lab?
A: No. Food and drinks are strictly not allowed inside the lab. A fine of Rs. 200 applies for violations.

Q: Is the lab open on Saturdays?
A: Only for final semester project students, from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

Q: What happens if I damage lab equipment?
A: The actual repair/replacement cost will be charged to the student if damage is due to negligence.
