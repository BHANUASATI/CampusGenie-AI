CENTRAL LIBRARY RULES

1. TIMINGS
Library working hours: 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM (Monday to Saturday)
Reading room (extended hours during exams): 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM
Sundays: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM (reading room only, no book issue)

2. BOOK ISSUE POLICY
2.1 MCA students can issue a maximum of 3 books at a time for a period of 14 days.
2.2 Renewal is allowed once for an additional 14 days if the book is not reserved by another student.
2.3 Fine for late return: Rs. 2 per day per book.
2.4 Reference books, journals, and back-year project reports are for in-library reading only and cannot be issued.
2.5 Final year students working on their major project can issue up to 5 books, and reference project reports for a maximum of 3 days for photocopying purposes.

3. DIGITAL LIBRARY ACCESS
3.1 Access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and Springer is available through the library portal using institutional login credentials.
3.2 e-books and previous year question papers can be downloaded from the student portal under "Library Resources".

4. LOST OR DAMAGED BOOKS
4.1 If a book is lost or damaged, the student must pay the current market price of the book plus a processing fee of Rs. 100.

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: How many books can I issue at a time?
A: MCA students can issue up to 3 books for 14 days (5 books for final year project students).
Q: What is the fine for a late return?
A: Rs. 2 per day per book.
Q: Can I access IEEE papers from the library?
A: Yes, via IEEE Xplore and ACM Digital Library through the library portal login.
