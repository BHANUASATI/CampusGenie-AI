CODE OF CONDUCT AND ANTI-RAGGING POLICY

1. GENERAL CODE OF CONDUCT
1.1 Students must carry their college ID card at all times within the campus.
1.2 Use of unfair means in examinations (copying, use of mobile phones, chits) will lead to cancellation of that examination and possible suspension for one semester on repeat offense.
1.3 Damage to college property will be charged to the responsible student(s); if the individual cannot be identified, the cost may be recovered from the entire class/batch.
1.4 Smoking, consumption of alcohol, or any banned substance is strictly prohibited anywhere on campus.

2. ANTI-RAGGING POLICY
2.1 Ragging in any form (verbal, physical, online/cyber) is a criminal offense as per UGC Regulations, 2009, and is strictly banned on campus and in hostels.
2.2 An Anti-Ragging Committee and Anti-Ragging Squad are constituted at the start of every academic year, chaired by the Principal/Director.
2.3 Any student found guilty of ragging may face: suspension from the institution, withholding of results, rustication for a period ranging from 1 semester to permanent expulsion, and reporting to local police.
2.4 Anti-Ragging helpline: Available 24x7 at the number displayed on the notice board and student portal; complaints can also be filed anonymously via the drop-box outside the Dean's office.
2.5 All new students and their parents must submit a signed anti-ragging affidavit at the time of admission (also available on the UGC anti-ragging portal www.antiragging.in).

3. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
3.1 The Student Grievance Redressal Committee meets on the first Monday of every month to address academic and non-academic complaints.
3.2 Grievances can be submitted in writing to the Grievance Cell or via the online grievance form on the student portal, and are typically resolved within 15 working days.

4. DRESS CODE
4.1 Students are expected to dress in a decent and formal/semi-formal manner during working hours; formal attire is mandatory during placement drives, seminars, and viva-voce examinations.

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: What should I do if I witness ragging?
A: Report immediately to the Anti-Ragging Squad, Warden, or use the 24x7 helpline. Complaints can also be made anonymously.
Q: What is the punishment for using unfair means in exams?
A: Cancellation of that examination; repeat offenses can lead to suspension for one semester.
